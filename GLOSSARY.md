# Segue

An Android client for Navidrome. The language below covers what plays, how it gets queued, how the
queue follows the user between devices, and what is available offline.

## Playing and queueing

**Queue**:
The ordered list of songs the player works through. Its order is always the play order.
_Avoid_: Playlist (a server-side list the user edits), play order, timeline

**Placement**:
Where newly queued songs go: replace the queue and play now, play next, or add to the end of the queue.
_Avoid_: Mode, insert type

**Play intent**:
A user's request to queue something: a list of songs, an album, an artist, a playlist or an album source, with a placement and whether to shuffle. An artist means their top songs, or all their albums when the server has no top songs. When a play intent queues nothing, the user is told why.
_Avoid_: Play request, command

**Shuffle**:
A change in order made once, when songs are queued. **Tracks** shuffle reorders songs; **albums** shuffle reorders whole albums and keeps each album's disc and track order. A running queue is never reshuffled.
_Avoid_: Shuffle mode

**Album source**:
Where a "Play albums", "Shuffle albums" or "Shuffle all" intent takes its albums from: the library's albums as sorted and filtered, an artist, a genre, a playlist, or the whole library. Every album it covers is queued at once; "Shuffle all" is a tracks shuffle of every song in the whole library.
_Avoid_: Album list, album feed

**Instant mix**:
A queue of songs similar to a seed song, artist or album, generated by the server.
_Avoid_: Radio, similar songs

## Queue sync

**Queue sync**:
Saving the queue to the server when it changes, and noticing on start, on return, or on play pressed outside the app that another client saved a newer one.
_Avoid_: Queue backup, polling

**Saved queue**:
A queue stored somewhere other than the player: on this device for restore after process death, or on the server by queue sync.
_Avoid_: Snapshot, backup

**Adoption**:
Replacing the local queue with another client's newer saved queue, either when the user taps *Continue from &lt;device&gt;* (in the banner or the notification) or automatically. It is adopted paused, and an automatic adoption can be undone, except when play pressed outside the app adopts it: then it plays at once. Continue in the notification also plays it.
_Avoid_: Import, takeover

## Offline

**Pin**:
A user's choice to keep an album, playlist, artist or song downloaded for offline listening.
_Avoid_: Favorite (that marks a song, album or artist the user likes), save

**Offline**:
The device has no validated internet connection, or the user turned on *Offline mode*. A server that is down while the network is fine does not count. Either way, only downloaded songs are available.
_Avoid_: Disconnected

**Available**:
A song that can play right now: always when online, and only when downloaded while offline. Unavailable songs are shown dimmed and are never queued.
_Avoid_: Playable, enabled
